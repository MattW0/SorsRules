During gameplay, you improve your [[Initial deck]] by buying [[Creature]] cards and [[Technology]] cards using [[Buys]]. You can later play these cards using [[Plays]]. Buying and playing cards costs money, which you pay with [[Cash]] earned by playing [[Money]] cards. _Cash_, _Buys_, _Plays_ and [[Prevails]] are resources you gain during a turn. 

During turns, you generate and spend four types of resources:
- [[Cash]] – used to pay costs
- [[Buys]] – buy cards from the [[Market]] during [[Invent]] and [[Recruit]]
- [[Plays]] – play cards from your [[Hand]] during [[Develop]] and [[Deploy]]
- [[Prevails]] – used only in the [[Prevail]] phase

All resources empty during at the end of a turn, but money empties at the end of each [[Phases|Phase]].

*Example*: at the beginning of the phase _Invent_ you get _+1 Buy_, which allows you to buy one Technology or one Creature during this turn.
