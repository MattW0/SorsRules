The battlefield consists of two zones with seven slots for [Creatures](Creatures) and and six slots for [Technologies](Technology). This means that you can have up to seven Creatures and up to six Technologies in play simultaneously. 

To play a [[Technology]] or [[Creature]] card, you have to spend a _[Play](Plays)_. That action creates the corresponding entity on the Battlefield. The card remains attached to that [[Entity]] and remains uninteractable, while the former is on the battlefield. Cards in the _Play Zone_ can't be interacted with and stay there until the corresponding entity dies. Once that happens, the entity is destroyed and the card moves to your **Discard** pile. 

The **Player/Opponent Stats** shows your and your opponent's stats, including the player name, current **Life Total**, **Points** and all resources gathered during the turn.
