A turn of _Sors_ is built on a selection of nine phases. Three of them are played every turn, while the other six are optional. At the beginning of the turn, players choose two out of the six optional phases. Both players always play ALL phases that ANY player picked as well as the three mandatory ones. At the beginning of a phase, each player gets an effect described in the table below. Everyone who picked an optional phase, also gets the bonus at the beginning of that phase. Most phases are split into distinct steps, more to those detail in the phase specific sections. The phases are played in the following order, skipping the optional ones that NEITHER player has picked.

| Phase                   | Fix | Action                                                                              | Bonus                            | Steps |
| ----------------------- | --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----- |
| **[[Phase Selection]]** | ✔   | +2 Cards, choose 2 phases for this turn                                             | -                                | 2     |
| **[[Draw]]**            | ❌   | +2 Cards, discard 1                                                                 | +2 Cards                         | 2     |
| **[[Invent]]**          | ❌   | Buy Technologies from the market                                                    | +1 Buy, Technologies cost 1 less | 1+    |
| **[[Develop]]**         | ❌   | Play Technologies                                                                   | +1 Play, +2 Cash                 | 1+    |
| **[[Combat]]**          | ✔   | Creatures may deal damgage (Attack step) and block opposing Attackers (Defend step) | -                                | 4     |
| **[[Recruit]]**         | ❌   | Buy Creatures from the market                                                       | +1 Buy, Creatures cost 1 less    | 1+    |
| **[[Deploy]]**          | ❌   | Play Creatures                                                                      | +1 Play, +2 Cash                 | 1+    |
| **[[Prevail]]**         | ❌   | +1 [Prevail](Prevails.md)                                                           | +2 Prevail                       | 1-4   |
| **[[Clean Up]]**        | ✔   | The board resets and players get ready for the next turn.                           | -                                | 1     |
