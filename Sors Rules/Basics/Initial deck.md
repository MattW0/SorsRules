Each player begins with a 10-card deck:
- **6 Money cards:** 6× _Paper Money_
- **2 Technology cards:** _Junk_, _Clue_
- **2 Creature cards:** _Thug_, _Worker_

Both players shuffle their deck and draw 5 cards as their starting hand.
