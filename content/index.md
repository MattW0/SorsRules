>[!danger] Setting
>In the cyberpunk dystopia of Nox City you are a prominent mob boss in the constant struggle of underground shenanigans. You look for allies and the market's next-gen technology to help you in this vicious bidding against the fierce competition. Their allegiance remains as long as the payments keep flowing, but you know that your choices echo in the neon-lit streets, where Money rules, trust is fragile, and the line between victory and defeat is razor-thin. The city's pulse beats with tension as you contemplate the next moves in this deadly game for control.

>[!info] Summary
> The first who reduces their opponent's [[Health]] to zero or accumulates ten [[Victory Points]], wins the game! You build a deck of [[Card]]s during playtime to beat your opponent in creature based battle. To improve your deck and the chances to win, spend [[Money]] to buy stronger ones from the [[Market]] and get rid of the bad initial cards and. Earn points and enhance your limited resources with [[Technology]] and play [[Creature]]s to attack your opponent.
