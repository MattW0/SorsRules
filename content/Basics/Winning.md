After the [[Queue]] empties, a player immediately wins if **either** of the following conditions is met:  
• The opponent has zero [[Health]].  
• The player has ten or more [[Victory Points]].

If a player has ten or more [[Victory Points]] **and** zero [[Health]], the opponent wins instead.

If **both players** reach zero [[Health]], determine the winner in the following order:
1. The player with more [[Victory Points]] wins.
2. If both players have ten or more [[Victory Points]] and the same total, the player with more [[Health]] wins.

If none of the above conditions determine a winner, the game ends in a draw.
