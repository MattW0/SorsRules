This multiplayer card and deck building game is inspired by the thought, that only informed decisions can defeat endless possibilities. Every game should start with an check on the [[Market]], since that influences the game start.

Sors is played **simultaneously.** In most situations, both players may act at the same time or skip. When one player completes an action or skips, they wait until the other player has also finished or skipped. Once both players are ready, play proceeds to the next action.

Such an action is usually one of the [[Phases]]. A player automatically skips that phase and action, if they have none available.

Either player [[Winning]] takes about 30 min and they will return to the [[Lobby]].
