You draw cards from your deck and keep them in hand until you play them or interact otherwise (discard, trash). During the phases [[Invent]], [[Recruit]], [[Develop]] and [[Deploy]] you play and buy cards respectively. To buy and play other cards you usually need [[Cash]] that you get form playing [[Money]] cards.

There are three types of cards:
- [[Technology]]
- [[Creature]]
- [[Money]]

When you play [Technologies](Technology.md) and [Creatures](Creature.md) from your hand, they become [Entities](Entity.md) on the [[Board/Battlefield]]. A [[Card]] and the corresponding entity are coupled and share the same stats amongst [[Cost]], [[Health]], [[Abilities]], and all other defining traits. The stats of a card may vary temporarily due to a [Phase](Phases) bonus or abilities. Similary, entities may have their stats changed, for example when taking damage. This does not change the base stats of the corresponding card. Many abilities only trigger while the entity is on the [[Board/Battlefield]], although there may be exceptions.

An entity dies when its health reaches zero. Then, it leaves the Battlefield and the corresponding card moves to your discard pile.
